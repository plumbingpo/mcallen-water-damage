Flood Damage Preparation Tips in McAllen, TX

Home / Blog / How to Prepare for Flooding in McAllen, TX

The weather is out of your hands, but you can act early to shield a property in McAllen, TX against flood damage. Being prepared significantly softens the blow of a flood and may save you thousands on restoration. This guide from McAllen Restoration covers the most effective preparation strategies for McAllen homeowners.

Understand the Flood Risk You Face

Understanding your property's flood risk is the first step in preparation. Look up your address on the FEMA flood maps to see if it falls inside a designated flood zone. Even if you're not in a high-risk zone, consider that most flood claims come from outside designated flood zones. Look at how your land slopes, how it drains, and how close it sits to any waterway that might overflow in heavy rain.

Protect Your Property

The main physical steps are to seal cracks in the foundation and apply a waterproof coating, grade the soil so it slopes away from the house, install a battery-backed sump pump and keep it maintained, clear the gutters and downspouts and carry their discharge 6 feet or more away from the foundation, raise HVAC equipment and electrical panels above likely flood levels, and store valuables and important documents above ground level.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

Flood damage caused by rising water is not covered under a standard homeowner's policy. Think about buying flood insurance from a private carrier or the NFIP well before a storm is forecast, because coverage typically starts only after a 30-day waiting period. Document your property and belongings with photos and video for insurance purposes. Store your policy paperwork where you can get to it, and learn your deductible and coverage limits.

Plan Your Emergency Response

Have a plan in place before flooding threatens. Know your evacuation routes and have a go-bag ready. Keep McAllen Restoration's number ((551) 365-8603) in your phone for immediate post-flood professional cleanup. Keep contact details for your insurance agent somewhere easy to find. When a flood is in the forecast, carry valuables upstairs, shut off utilities if you are told to, and record the condition of your property before it arrives.
